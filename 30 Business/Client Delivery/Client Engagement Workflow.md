# Client Engagement Workflow

Internal proposal for Rarticle Software Sdn. Bhd. This is not an approved client policy or a signed agreement.

> [!warning] Commercial terms need a decision
> The original proposal includes an unconditional full refund at the first milestone and different refund terms later. Keep these as proposed terms until ZP approves the exact contract.

## Proposed workflow

1. Meet or call the client to align on the problem, requirements, and goals. Use multiple discovery sessions when needed.
2. Propose the solution, quotation, and milestones once the client's expectations are clear. Amendments are allowed before the contract is confirmed and signed.
3. After the contract and milestones are signed and the 20% upfront payment is received, complete the proposed legal process and agree on delivery dates for each milestone. Begin implementation and report progress. The original phrase “legal progress” still needs a definition.
4. Deliver the first milestone for the client's acceptance check. Address defects and feedback. Material scope changes may incur extra charges. The original proposal allows a full refund at this stage without giving a reason.
5. For the second and later milestones, collect the corresponding payment after delivery and acceptance. Move disputed or incomplete items to an agreed later milestone, or arrange another acceptance check after fixes. From the second stage, the original proposal makes the upfront payment non-refundable on termination, or partly refundable according to the contract and work delivered. The exact boundary remains undecided.
6. After the final acceptance check is completed without unresolved issues, collect the remaining balance within 14 days. Provide the proposed 3–6 months of defect and bug fixes. Handle new or changed requirements through a new contract and delivery process.

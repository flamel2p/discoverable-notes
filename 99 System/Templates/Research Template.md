# Research Template

Use this template for dated research with traceable sources.

> [!tip] Before using this template
> Rename the file, title property, and H1. Change `type` to `research`, `status` to `draft`, `verification` to `unverified`, and `updated` to the edit date. Add `as_of` for dated research.

State the research question and the date or period covered.

## Findings

Keep reported facts and their citations separate from inference and recommendation.

## Assumptions and limitations

State missing evidence and unresolved disagreements.

## Sources

Record the source title, link, publication date or version, and the claims it supports.

## Related

Add links to the topic index and relevant notes.
